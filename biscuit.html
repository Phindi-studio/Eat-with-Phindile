Easy Butter Biscuits

Serves: 6–8

Prep Time: 15 minutes

Cook Time: 20 minutes

These soft and buttery homemade biscuits are simple to make and perfect for any occasion. With just a few ingredients, you can create a delicious treat that everyone will love.

Ingredients

  • 500g butter (room temperature)
  • 1 ¼ cups sugar
  • 4 eggs
  • 6 cups flour

Instructions

  1. Add butter to a bowl (make sure it is at room temperature).
  2. Add sugar and mix until smooth and creamy.
  3. Whisk eggs separately, then gradually add to the mixture.
  4. Add flour and mix until a soft dough forms.
  5. Shape biscuits and place on a baking tray.
  6. Bake until golden brown.

Tip: For best results, don’t overmix the dough.
